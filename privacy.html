Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Justice Scanner is published by Jasper Fernald ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: the app has no accounts, shows no ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera. The camera is used only to read barcodes. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your first name, if you choose to give one. It is used to address you in the app and never leaves your phone.
  • Your scan history. It is stored on your phone and removed when you delete the app.
  • Reminders. If you allow notifications, the weekly reminder is scheduled on your phone. We do not send push messages from a server.

What leaves your phone

  • Barcode numbers. When you scan a product, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts and its sister databases (Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Pet Food Facts) to find the product's name and brand. They receive the barcode number and, as with any internet request, your IP address. Their privacy policy is at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are bought through Apple. We never see your card or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.
  • Paywall service. We use Superwall to show the subscription screen and to know whether a subscription is active. Superwall receives a random identifier created for your installation of the app, your subscription status, basic device information (such as model, operating system version, language and country), and the answers you tap during setup: what you want to avoid, the shops you picked, the weekly amounts you set, and where you heard about the app. It does not receive your name. Superwall's privacy policy is at superwall.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or share information with advertisers.
  • We do not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts or location.

Why we use this information

To look up the products you scan, to provide the subscription you paid for, and to understand in aggregate which setup screens and subscription screens work.

How long it is kept

Information on your phone stays until you delete the app. Information held by Superwall is tied to a random identifier, not to your name, and is kept under their retention policy.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without giving a name, without allowing notifications, and without allowing the camera (you can search by brand name instead).
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
  • To ask what is held against your installation's identifier, or to have it deleted, email us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access, correct or delete your information; we will honour those requests.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
