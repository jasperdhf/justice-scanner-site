What the app is

Justice Scanner lets you scan a product's barcode or search for a brand to see which company is behind it and how that company is rated in our guide. Ratings are our editorial judgement, based on the public sources listed with each rating and the date each was checked.

Accuracy

Results are estimates worked out automatically from public records that have not been individually reviewed, such as trademark registers, business directories and where a barcode was issued. They show what is on record, not a verified conclusion about a company. Company ownership and activity change, records contain errors, and a product can be matched to the wrong brand or company. A product with no records is shown as "No records", which is not a statement that it is clear. Use the app as one input to your own decisions. If you believe a result is wrong, use "Report a problem with this result" in the app or email us, and we will review it.
